What it does

Built around the throw down.

Mark catch and release

Load a clip and tag 1st catch, release, and 2nd catch at the bag. Pop time and exchange drop out of those frames — to second or third.

Frame-by-frame editor

Scrub every frame, including high-fps film up to 240. The export keeps the original frame rate.

Sound visualization

When the catcher’s back is to the camera, use the waveform to mark pitch reception you cannot see.

Export with overlay

Save the marked video with pop and exchange burned in. Overlay position is customizable. Screen stays awake until the file is written.

Receiving mode

Glove trajectory and a strike-zone overlay so you can break down every catch, not just the throw down.

Pitcher mode & velocity

A dedicated pitcher mode plus velo from the same clip — no radar gun.

Footwork tracking

Pro users can mark footwork on the throw down, not only hands and pop.

Coach mode

A separate subscription tier with extra overlay controls for coaches working a roster.

How it works

Three marks. One number.

  1. 01

    Pick a clip

    POPtime only sees the video you choose. No full photo-library access. Export asks for add-only permission.

  2. 02

    Mark the frames

    Catch. Release. Optional 2nd catch at the bag. Scroll frames or use the waveform when the glove is hidden.

  3. 03

    Read and share

    Pop time, exchange, overlay export. Same numbers on every replay.
